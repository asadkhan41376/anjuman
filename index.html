Who We Are

About Anjuman Ta'limul Islam

Anjuman Ta’limul Islam, Udaipur is one of the region’s oldest and most respected educational and social institutions, founded in 1902 and officially registered in 1942. Established with the vision of promoting education, social reform, and community upliftment, Anjuman has played a vital role in shaping generations through guidance, discipline, and collective progress.

With a strong foundation rooted in values such as education, unity, empowerment, inclusiveness, and responsibility, Anjuman continues to serve as a trusted platform for individuals seeking growth, awareness, and direction. Over the decades, it has contributed significantly to educational development and social welfare, making it a recognized name in Udaipur and surrounding regions.

While primarily focused on the betterment of the Muslim community, Anjuman also actively encourages participation from people of all backgrounds in its general initiatives, reflecting its inclusive and community-oriented approach.

Today, Anjuman Ta’limul Islam remains committed to its vision of guiding youth towards the right path, empowering individuals with knowledge and skills, and building a society that is educated, self-reliant, and progressive.

  • Established in 1902 | Registered in 1942
  • Among the oldest Muslim institutions in the region
  • Strong focus on education, unity, and empowerment
  • Active role in social welfare and community development
  • Vision-driven approach towards youth growth and self-reliance

Our Inspiration

Hazrat Mufti Zahirul Hasan Sahab (R.A.)

The Founder & Guiding Visionary

Hazrat Mufti Zahirul Hasan Sahab Rahmatullahi Alaih, widely known as “Bade Maulana Sahab”, was a distinguished Islamic scholar, reformer, and visionary leader who founded Anjuman Ta’limul Islam, Udaipur in 1902.

Born on 1st Shawwal 1302 Hijri (1881 AD), he pursued his early education in Aurangabad and later attained advanced Islamic knowledge under the guidance of great scholars, including A’ala Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan (R.A.), becoming his Khalifa. His expertise extended across both religious and rational sciences, reflecting his deep scholarship and intellectual strength.

Born on 1st Shawwal 1302 Hijri (1881 AD), he pursued his early education in Aurangabad and later attained advanced Islamic knowledge under the guidance of great scholars, including A’ala Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan (R.A.), becoming his Khalifa. His expertise extended across both religious and rational sciences, reflecting his deep scholarship and intellectual strength.

Upon arriving in Udaipur, he dedicated his life to the service of Deen and community, spending over 50 years in education, reform, and social welfare. His vision was to build an educated, disciplined, and aware society rooted in strong values.

Hazrat Mufti Zahirul Hasan Sahab (R.A.) passed away on 12 Rabi-ul-Sharif 1382 Hijri (14 August 1962). His blessed mazaar is located at the Astana of Hazrat Imarat Rasool Baba (R.A.), Udaipur, where devotees continue to pay their respects.

His legacy lives on through Anjuman Ta’limul Islam, which continues to serve as a center of knowledge, guidance, and community development.

Key Highlights

  • Founder of Anjuman Ta’limul Islam (1902)
  • Known as Bade Maulana Sahab
  • Khalifa of A’ala Hazrat Imam Ahmad Raza Khan (R.A.)
  • Served the community for over 50 years
  • Scholar of both religious and rational sciences
  • Mazar at Astana of Hazrat Imarat Rasool Baba (R.A.), Udaipur
